About me

Efficient AI, built to run in the real world

I'm an AI engineer with 3+ years of experience across LLMs, RAG, computer vision and full-stack development. I hold a Master's in Artificial Intelligence & Virtual Reality from Ibn Tofail University, where my thesis studied adaptive generation of structured data with LLMs, with a focus on frugality and evaluation.

I care about models that are fast and light as well as accurate. That means LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning, INT4/INT8 quantization, pruning, and measuring latency and energy alongside quality, then shipping the result with Docker.
